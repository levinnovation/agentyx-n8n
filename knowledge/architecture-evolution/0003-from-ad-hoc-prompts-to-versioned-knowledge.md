# 0003: From ad-hoc prompts to versioned knowledge

## Before

Important reasoning lived in chat transcripts, tickets, or one-off docs—hard to find and easy to contradict.

## After

Durable knowledge under `knowledge/`:

- ADRs for decisions
- Prompt logs for AI sessions
- Change records for implementation history
- Context packs for reusable agent bootstrap

## Why it matters

Onboarding and audits improve; agents and humans share the same canonical narrative.
