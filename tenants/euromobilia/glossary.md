| Electrodoméstico | Home appliance (refrigerator, oven, dishwasher, etc.) |
| Mueble de cocina | Kitchen cabinet or furniture piece |
| Cotización | Quotation / price estimate |
| Kapso | WhatsApp Business API provider used by Euromobilia |
| Bitrix24 | CRM used for lead and deal tracking |
