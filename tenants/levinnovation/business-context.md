# LEV Innovation Business Context

## Overview

LEV Innovation builds AI-powered automation for growth, operations, and customer
engagement. This tenant is used for internal go-to-market execution, with a
focus on deterministic AI-assisted sales prospecting.

Primary target account discovery source for v1 is LinkedIn company and people
data through RapidAPI-compatible providers.

## Key Processes

1. Lead sourcing from LinkedIn search endpoints.
2. Automated lead qualification and personalized outreach draft generation.
3. Lead registration and lifecycle tracking in HubSpot.
4. Meeting slot proposal based on Google Calendar availability.
5. Internal notifications and execution handoffs through Slack.

## Ideal Customer Profile (initial)

- SMB and mid-market teams evaluating AI automation.
- Decision makers in operations, sales, and customer success.
- Companies with repetitive workflows and clear revenue/efficiency targets.

## Success Metrics

- Qualified lead rate >= 30% of sourced leads.
- Outreach send rate >= 90% for qualified leads with email.
- Median first-response time <= 24 hours.
- Meeting conversion rate >= 10% from qualified leads.
